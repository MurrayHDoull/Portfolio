Steel Building Design & Adaptive Reuse

Group Coursework University of Sheffield First Class

This group design project involved the complete structural design of a multi-storey steel frame building for mixed-use occupancy, incorporating adaptive reuse principles and sustainability considerations.

I led structural calculations for floor beams, columns, roof trusses, and lateral stability systems, applying British Standards and Eurocodes throughout. The project required balancing structural performance, constructability, and environmental impact, including appraisal of existing building capacity and design of new structural elements to accommodate load increases.

Outcome

The work demonstrates the ability to collaborate effectively in technical teams, develop creative engineering solutions under real-world constraints, and communicate complex structural concepts through professional drawings and calculations. Awarded First Class grade for innovative problem-solving and exceptional presentation quality.
