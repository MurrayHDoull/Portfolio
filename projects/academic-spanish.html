Academic Achievement in Spanish

Year Abroad Granada, Spain Eurocode-based study

Studying engineering in Spain required mastering complex technical content entirely in Spanish, pushing the development of precise communication and disciplined independent learning.

Completed modules in structural mechanics, materials, and design, adapting quickly to new teaching methods, terminology, and assessment styles while operating outside a familiar linguistic comfort zone.

Outcome

This experience strengthened the ability to understand, explain, and apply engineering concepts across languages, and helped build resilience, accuracy, and focus under pressure. Academic performance improved steadily throughout the year, reflecting determination to engage fully with the Spanish academic system and broaden a structural engineering perspective through exposure to Eurocode-based design approaches.
