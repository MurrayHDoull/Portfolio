Reinforced Concrete Design (Beam / Slab / Column)

Coursework · Revit University of Sheffield First Class

This project involved the complete structural design of a multi-storey building, including columns, beams, and slabs designed to Eurocode standards.

I conducted load calculations, analysed structural behaviour under various load combinations, and sized all primary structural elements to ensure code compliance and structural integrity. The design was developed and documented in Revit, producing a full set of structural drawings and calculations.

Outcome

This work demonstrates the ability to apply structural engineering principles in practice, work within industry-standard design codes, and use professional BIM software to deliver coordinated structural solutions. Awarded a First Class grade, reflecting thorough technical execution and clear communication of design decisions.
