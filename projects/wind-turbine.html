Wind Turbine Project — Engineers Without Borders

Interdisciplinary · EWB Whirlow Farm, Sheffield 30-student team

This interdisciplinary engineering project involved designing and constructing a 2.4m diameter, 700W wind turbine for Whirlow Farm as part of a 30-student collaborative team.

Working on the mechanical design team as the structural engineer, I developed detailed 3D CAD models in AutoCAD to validate the turbine design and support fabrication. The project required close coordination across electrical, mechanical, and civil engineering disciplines, translating theoretical knowledge into practical application.

Outcome

This hands-on experience demonstrates the ability to work effectively in large, diverse teams and apply structural principles to real-world renewable energy systems.
